# Credits

Nimbledeck exists because of work other people gave away. None of what you see here would have been possible without it, and we
are grateful to every one of these projects and people.

## The ground it stands on

- **[Slidev](https://sli.dev)** by Anthony Fu and the Slidev team: the deck engine. Markdown slides, layouts, addons and themes
  are theirs, and Nimbledeck is a layer on top of that idea. Nimbledeck is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
- **[Vue](https://vuejs.org)**: every live component.
- **[KaTeX](https://katex.org)**: the equations, set from LaTeX in the Markdown.
- **[three.js](https://threejs.org)**: the 3D scenes.
- **[Chart.js](https://www.chartjs.org)** and **[Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org)**: charts and diagrams.
- **[CodeMirror](https://codemirror.net)**: the editor of the live-code slide and of the playground.
- **[Fuse.js](https://www.fusejs.io)**: the fuzzy search.
- **[marimo](https://marimo.io)**, **[NumPy](https://numpy.org)**, **[matplotlib](https://matplotlib.org)** and
  **[websockets](https://websockets.readthedocs.io)**: the Python side of the demos and streams.
- **[Playwright](https://playwright.dev)** and **[FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org)**: export, recordings and their encoding.
- **[Zensical](https://zensical.org)**, by the makers of Material for MkDocs: this documentation site.
- **[uv](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/)** from Astral: Python that installs itself.

## Music

## The science deck

The science deck stands on the shoulders of people whose ideas it only retells: Thomas Bayes and Richard Price, and the
researchers of the papers in [`examples/science/REFERENCES.md`](https://github.com/arnaudbletterer/nimbledeck/blob/main/examples/science/REFERENCES.md),
each checked against its Crossref or PubMed record.
